Event-Based Vision Sensor

Unlike conventional cameras that capture full frames at fixed intervals (30–60 FPS), an Event-Based Vision Sensor (EVS) mimics biological retinas — each pixel reacts autonomously and asynchronously only when it detects a change in logarithmic illumination.
